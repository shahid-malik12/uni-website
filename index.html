Vice Chancellor's Message

It is indeed an honor for me to be the Vice Chancellor of the University of Layyah (UL), Layyah. It was my passion to lead an institute of higher learning engaged in enlightening the youth to have a well-lived life along with opportunities for entrepreneurship and employment.

I will try to contribute all my efforts to make this University the best one by enabling students and faculty through modern teaching methods, practical education & collaborative research across the spectrum of challenges and opportunities prevailing over the globe.

I welcome you to learn, explore and grow with the latest, updated and sustainability-oriented knowledge and skills to meet the competitive challenges of this modern era.

I wish you a successful and promising educational journey with UL open to all genders and the students of all areas!

Prof. Dr. Muhammad Zubair Abu Bakar

Vice Chancellor, University of Layyah
